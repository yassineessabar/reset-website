How to book a reset

  • Get started

    Enter your address in the RESET app, pick a time and confirm.

  • Get matched with a RESET member

    A background-checked RESET member accepts your visit. You’ll see their name and photo before they arrive.

  • Hand over your keys

    Leave a key or a door code. We return it the moment the visit is done.

  • Get on with your day

    We reset your home room by room while you’re out. Follow along live in the app.

  • Come home to a note

    “All done. You can breathe.” Rate your visit and add a tip if you like.
