Questions from future RESET members

Do I need experience?

Experience helps, but it is not required. We show you how RESET works before your first visit.

Do I need my own supplies?

No. We supply the kit you need for every visit.

How do I choose when I work?

Go online in the RESET Team app and accept the visits that suit your week.

How do I get paid?

You get paid for every visit you complete, tips included, straight to your account.

What checks are there?

We confirm your identity and run a background check before your first visit.
