How it works

  • Choose an amount

    From $50, or any amount from $25 to $1,000.

  • Add a message

    We email the gift card now or on the day you choose.

  • They book a reset

    The balance goes towards any visit until it is used.

Gift card questions

How long is a gift card valid?

At least three years from the date you buy it, as Australian law requires.

What can it be used for?

Any reset: a one-off clean, a deep reset, a move in or out, or recurring visits until the balance runs out.

Can I choose when it arrives?

Yes. Send it now, tomorrow or on a date you choose, with your own message.

Can a gift card be refunded?

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.
